Tenfold privacy policy

Mental maths trainer for Android · Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short: Tenfold does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not use the internet. Everything it remembers stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

To keep your place between sessions, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your progress: level, experience points, streaks and personal bests;
  • how you have done on each skill, which the app uses to choose questions of the right difficulty;
  • a short summary of recent sets (date, score and time), kept for up to 90 days;
  • your daily challenge results;
  • your choice of look (light or dark), and whether you agreed to reminders.

This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request the internet permission.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files or microphone or camera data.
  • It does not use advertising identifiers, trackers, analytics or advertising of any kind.
  • It does not sell or share information with anyone, because it holds none that is sent anywhere.

Notifications

After you have completed a few sets, the app may ask whether you would like a gentle daily reminder. This is optional. If you agree, Android asks for permission to show notifications, and the app schedules one short reminder a day on your phone itself. No server is involved. You can turn the reminders off at any time in your phone's notification settings for the app.

Sharing your daily challenge result

If you tap Share on a daily challenge result, the app passes a short piece of text (for example the score and a row of coloured squares) to Android's share menu. You choose whether and where to send it. The app does not see or control what happens after that.

Children

Tenfold is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing from children, or anyone else, to collect.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may process information about the installation under its own privacy policy. That is separate from the app, which receives none of it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The app will not start collecting data without this policy saying so first.
